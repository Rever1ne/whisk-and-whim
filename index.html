01
How a pull worksTwo spins, one final milkshake.
1Choose your Konling username so your progress is kept separate.
2Spin the rarity wheel using the published permanent rates.
3The matching milkshake wheel opens automatically for ★–★★★★. A ★★★★★ pull reveals Konkon Dreams immediately.
4For ★–★★★★, spin again. Every milkshake inside the selected rarity has an equal chance.
02
Standard rarity ratesThese rates apply whenever a guarantee is not active.
03
★★★+ guaranteeYour low-rarity streak can never go past four.
After 4 consecutive ★ / ★★ pulls, your next rarity pull is guaranteed to be ★★★ or higher.

A natural ★★★, ★★★★ or ★★★★★ pull resets the counter immediately. A guaranteed ★★★+ pull also resets it after the pull is completed.

On a guaranteed pull, the rarity wheel itself changes to show only ★★★ / ★★★★ / ★★★★★. Their normal weights are rebalanced across those three tiers.

04
Duplicates & Syrup DropsDuplicates still give value.

The first time you reveal a milkshake it is marked NEW. Any later copy is marked DUPLICATE and gives Syrup Drops based on its rarity.

05
Full permanent shake poolEach rarity shows its real pull rate. Milkshake percentages show the chance inside that rarity after it has been selected.
06
Reward rulesThese rules apply to all milkshake rewards.

Discount rewards: discounts are non-stackable. Only one discount reward can be used on a single order or session.

Bonus minutes: any reward that adds extra minutes must be attached to a paid game/session order. Bonus minutes cannot be redeemed as a standalone free session.

07
Tracking & randomnessWhat the wheel is actually doing behind the animation.

Random selection: results are chosen centrally with a cryptographic server-side random generator. The wheel animation displays the selected result; it does not decide the odds visually.

Per-Konling progress: total pulls, unique shakes, Syrup Drops, pull history and guarantee progress are tracked separately for each Konling username.

Current storage: authoritative pulls, pity and Syrup Drops are stored centrally. This browser keeps a local mirror for faster display and resyncs known Konlings from the central log.

Rewards: the milkshake reward field is still being finalized. When a reward is assigned, it will appear on that shake's reveal card. The global reward rules above apply to every assigned reward.
