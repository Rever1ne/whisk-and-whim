Probability information

Pull Details

The permanent rarity rates are fixed. After four consecutive ★ / ★★ pulls, the next rarity pull is guaranteed to be ★★★ or higher. A ★★★+ pull resets that customer's guarantee progress. After the rarity is selected, every shake inside that rarity has an equal chance of being selected.

Reward rules

Discount rewards: discounts are non-stackable. Only one discount reward can be used on a single order or session.

Bonus minutes: any reward that adds extra minutes must be attached to a paid game/session order. Bonus minutes cannot be redeemed as a standalone free session.
